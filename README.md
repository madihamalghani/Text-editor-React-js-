# Multi-Theme Text Editor

A sleek, user-friendly **multi-theme text editor** designed to enhance your writing experience with versatile text manipulation features and customizable appearance.

---

## Features

### 1. **Multiple Themes**

- **Default White Theme**: Clean and simple for everyday use.
- **Dark Theme**: Eye-friendly dark mode to reduce eye strain during night-time editing.
- **Blue Theme**: A calm blue color scheme to boost focus and creativity.
- **Green Theme**: A fresh green interface for a refreshing look.

Users can easily switch between these themes to suit their mood or environment.

---

### 2. **Text Manipulation Tools**

- **Convert to Sentence Case**: Automatically formats your text so that the first letter of each sentence is capitalized.
- **Capitalize**: Capitalizes the first letter of each word.
- **Uppercase**: Converts all text to uppercase letters.
- **Lowercase**: Converts all text to lowercase letters.
- **Copy Text**: Quickly copies the entire content to your clipboard.
- **Clear Text**: Clears all the content from the editor in a single click.

---

### 3. **Real-Time Text Statistics**

- **Word Count**: Displays the total number of words in the editor.
- **Estimated Reading Time**: Shows the approximate time it would take to read the text aloud.
- **Preview**: Live preview section that displays how the text looks after applying the chosen transformations.

---

### 4. **About Page**

An informative section featuring an accordion component with **3 frequently asked questions (FAQs)**. Each question can be expanded or collapsed to reveal detailed answers, helping users understand the purpose and functionality of the text editor.

## How to Use

1. **Type or paste your text** into the editor.
2. **Choose a theme** from the available options (White, Dark, Blue, Green).
3. Use the **buttons to manipulate your text**: sentence case, capitalize, uppercase, lowercase, copy, or clear.
4. **View live statistics** such as word count and estimated reading time below the editor.
5. Visit the **About page** to get quick answers to common questions about the editor.
